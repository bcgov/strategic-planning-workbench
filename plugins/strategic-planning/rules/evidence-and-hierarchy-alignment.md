# Rule: Evidence Integrity & Multi-Tier Organizational Alignment

## 1. The Iron Law
> **Every strategic artifact MUST separate confirmed evidence from unproven assumptions and trace team-level objectives upward through the organizational hierarchy.** Agents MUST NEVER fabricate mandates, baselines, or stakeholder positions.

## 2. Invariants & Forbidden Actions

1. **Multi-Tier Mandate Hierarchy**: Strategic context and alignment matrices MUST distinguish direction across all five public-sector organizational tiers:
   - **Tier 1 — Province-Wide (BC Government)**: Provincial strategic plan, mandate letters, Digital Plan, and DRIPA / Indigenous reconciliation commitments.
   - **Tier 2 — Ministry Level (Ministry of Attorney General)**: Ministry Service Plan, justice sector priorities, access to justice, and judicial independence guardrails.
   - **Tier 3 — Branch Level (Court Services Branch / CSB)**: CSB strategic vision, court operations, registry modernization, and sheriff/security mandates.
   - **Tier 4 — Division / Directorate Level**: Executive Director and division leadership direction, priorities, and resource bounds.
   - **Tier 5 — Team / Unit Level**: Team vision, strategic objectives, business capabilities, idea inventory, and committed work inventory.
2. **Confirmed vs. Interpreted Direction**: Every leadership statement or driver MUST be tagged as `Confirmed` (documented mandate/decision), `Interpreted` (inferred from remarks), or `Unvalidated Assumption`.
3. **Zero Fabricated Baselines**: Agents MUST NEVER invent baseline metrics, target percentages, or stakeholder consensus. Missing data MUST be logged in the Assumptions & Evidence Register as `Baseline Needed` or `Evidence Gap`.
4. **Judicial & Operational Independence**: Strategy artifacts in justice contexts MUST NEVER blur executive/administrative operations with judicial independence or adjudicative authority.
5. **No Premature Solutioning**: Agents MUST NOT prescribe software tools, vendor platforms, or project schedules before strategic context, outcome objectives, and required business capabilities are established.

## 3. Evaluation Checklist

- Are team objectives explicitly traced upward to Division, Branch (CSB), Ministry (AG), and Provincial mandates?
- Is every leadership statement and current-state claim tagged as `Confirmed`, `Interpreted`, or `Unvalidated Assumption`?
- Are all missing metrics recorded as `Baseline Needed` rather than fabricated numbers?
- Are disguised technology projects reframed into outcome-oriented objectives or routed to the Idea Inventory?
