# Analyzing strategic context

Guides non-technical and technical users through an interactive discovery conversation to establish leadership direction, map stakeholders ("the players"), assess current-state realities, and log assumptions and evidence gaps.

## Contents

## Constraints

1. **Interactive HITL pacing**: Ask **one or two plain-language questions at a time** or present a **numbered option menu (3–5 choices with a recommended default)**. Never overwhelm the user with a wall of questions.
2. **Evidence over invention**: Separate confirmed leadership statements and statutes (`Confirmed`) from team interpretations (`Interpreted`) and unproven beliefs (`Unvalidated Assumption`).
3. **B.C. Government Design System voice**: Use plain, direct language, sentence case for all headings and labels, zero emoji, and zero exclamation marks.
4. **Recap confirmation gate**: Present a concise recap summary and ask for confirmation (`yes / adjust`) before saving files to `strategy/`.

## Quick start

1. Read [context-and-stakeholder-guide.md](references/context-and-stakeholder-guide.md) and [public-sector-context-lens.md](references/public-sector-context-lens.md).
2. Greet the user with the **3-option intake menu** from [context-and-stakeholder-guide.md](references/context-and-stakeholder-guide.md) (1. Paste/upload notes, 2. Guided step-by-step interview, 3. Review/update existing `strategy/` context).
3. After recap confirmation, populate [strategic-context-brief.md](assets/templates/strategic-context-brief.md), [stakeholder-map.md](assets/templates/stakeholder-map.md), and [assumptions-and-evidence-register.md](assets/templates/assumptions-and-evidence-register.md).

## Workflow

1. **Interactive Intake & Leadership Direction**: Ask what Provincial, Ministry (Attorney General), Branch (Court Services Branch), or Divisional leaders (e.g., Chris, Jessica) have communicated. Classify each point as `Confirmed`, `Interpreted`, or `Assumption`.
2. **Guided Stakeholder ("Players") Mapping**: Ask who is affected or holds influence (e.g., judiciary, court clerks, sheriffs, legal aid, public users), what they care about, and where perspectives conflict using [stakeholder-map.md](assets/templates/stakeholder-map.md).
3. **Current-State Conversation**: Walk through services, workflows, information/technology, and workforce pain points one topic at a time, tagging `Evidence-Backed` vs. `Anecdotal`.
4. **Recap Gate & Save**: Summarize captured context, assumptions, and evidence gaps ([assumptions-and-evidence-register.md](assets/templates/assumptions-and-evidence-register.md)), confirm with the user, and offer a numbered next-step menu leading to `defining-strategic-vision`.
