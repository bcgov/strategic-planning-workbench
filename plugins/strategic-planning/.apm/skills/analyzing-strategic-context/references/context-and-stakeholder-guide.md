# Context, stakeholder and current-state analysis guide

## 1. Interactive intake menu (for Claude Cowork & GitHub Copilot users)

When a user starts a session without pasting full notes, open with this numbered menu:

```text
How would you like to capture your strategic context today?
1. Guided conversation — I will ask 1–2 plain-language questions at a time (Recommended)
2. Paste or upload notes — Share emails, meeting notes, or briefing decks from leadership and I will organize them
3. Stakeholder ("players") mapping first — Start by mapping the key people, groups, and their concerns
4. Review existing files — Check what is already recorded in your strategy/ folder
```

## 2. Progressive interview prompts (ask 1–2 at a time)

- **Step A — Upstream leadership direction**:
  - *"To start, what have your division or branch leaders (for example, Chris or Jessica) shared about where the team needs to focus? Feel free to share both formal direction and informal comments."*
  - Follow-up: *"Which of those points are confirmed mandates versus things we are reading between the lines?"*
- **Step B — Business drivers (problems and opportunities)**:
  - *"What are the top 2 or 3 pressures, bottlenecks, or opportunities pushing your team to act right now?"*
- **Step C — Stakeholders ("the players")**:
  - *"Who are the key players involved or affected (for example, registry staff, judiciary, partner branches, or public service users), and where do their priorities or concerns pull in different directions?"*
- **Step D — Current state and evidence gaps**:
  - *"Looking at how your team operates today, what works well and where are the biggest day-to-day pain points? Do we have data for those, or are they mainly observations so far?"*

## 3. Recap confirmation gate (before writing files)

Before saving to `strategy/01-org-hierarchy-and-context.md`, `strategy/02-stakeholders-and-concerns.md`, or `strategy/06-constraints-assumptions-risks.md`, present:

```text
Here is a recap of what I captured:
- Confirmed direction: [...]
- Interpreted / assumed direction: [...]
- Key stakeholders and tensions: [...]
- Current-state pain points and evidence gaps: [...]

Does this look accurate before I save it to your strategy registers?
1. Yes, save and continue to defining our target vision (Recommended)
2. Adjust a few details first
3. Show me a preview of the tables inline
```

## 4. Separating confirmed direction from interpretation

| Classification | Definition | Action |
|---|---|---|
| **Confirmed direction** | Explicitly stated in written mandate letters, executive decisions, statutes, or verified leadership communications. | Use as a foundational constraint or driver. |
| **Interpreted direction** | Inferred from leadership remarks, town halls, or informal conversations where exact scope or priority remains ambiguous. | Document the interpretation and list a clarifying question for leadership. |
| **Unverified assumption** | A belief held by the team without direct source evidence. | Log in the Assumptions and evidence register with a validation owner. |
