# Public-Sector & Justice/Court Services Context Lens

Apply this lens when analyzing strategic context for a government ministry, division, or justice/court services branch:

## Key Contextual Dimensions

1. **Legislative & Policy Mandates**: Statutory obligations, ministerial service plan commitments, and regulatory constraints that cannot be traded off like commercial product features.
2. **Judicial & Operational Independence**: In court and justice contexts, clearly separate administrative/executive branch responsibilities from judicial independence and adjudicative discretion.
3. **Access to Justice & Service Inclusion**: Evaluate how current-state barriers affect self-represented litigants, rural/remote communities, people with disabilities, and vulnerable populations.
4. **Indigenous Reconciliation Commitments**: Align context analysis with UNDRIP / DRIPA commitments, Indigenous justice strategies, and culturally safe service delivery.
5. **Privacy, Security & Records Management**: Account for FOIPPA/privacy legislation, court record access rules, information security classifications, and statutory retention schedules.
6. **Public Trust & Procedural Fairness**: Recognize that transparency, auditability, and procedural fairness carry equal or greater weight than speed or cost reduction.
7. **Cross-Ministry & Sector Dependencies**: Map dependencies on central IT, procurement, legal counsel, policing, corrections, legal aid, and external justice partners.
