# Capability-Based Planning Guide

## 1. Why Capabilities Are the Missing Bridge

Jumping directly from "who are the players?" or "what is our vision?" to a list of projects skips the foundational question:
> **"What capabilities must our organization possess or strengthen in order to achieve this future state?"**

Business capabilities remain relatively stable even when organizational structures, leadership teams, or technology platforms change.

## 2. Naming & Structuring Business Capabilities

| Anti-Pattern (Do Not Use) | Why It Fails | Compliant Business Capability Name |
|---|---|---|
| "SharePoint Online Migration" | Transient project / technology product | **Authoritative Operational Knowledge Management** |
| "Kyron's Analytics Team" | Org-chart unit / person | **Operational Performance & Demand Analytics** |
| "Copilot Studio Agent" | Vendor tool | **Guided Policy & Procedure Triage** |

## 3. Five Dimensions of a Business Capability

When assessing a capability gap, evaluate all five pillars:
1. **People & Skills**: Roles, competencies, training, and capacity.
2. **Process & Practice**: Standard workflows, playbooks, and quality assurance.
3. **Information & Data**: Data standards, metadata, authoritative sources, and records integrity.
4. **Technology & Tools**: Platforms, integrations, and security controls.
5. **Policy & Governance**: Decision rights, statutory compliance, privacy guardrails, and ownership.

## 4. Capability Maturity Scale (1–5)

- **Level 0 (Absent)**: Capability does not exist today.
- **Level 1 (Ad-Hoc / Heroic)**: Relies on individual heroics and informal tribal knowledge; undocumented.
- **Level 2 (Repeatable)**: Basic practices exist within pockets of the team but vary across units.
- **Level 3 (Defined & Standardized)**: Documented, governed, and consistently executed across the branch.
- **Level 4 (Measured & Managed)**: Backed by reliable operational metrics, service levels, and feedback loops.
- **Level 5 (Optimized & Adaptive)**: Continuously improved and shared as a reusable pattern across divisions.
