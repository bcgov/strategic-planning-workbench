# Acceptance criteria: `developing-and-managing-strategy` (Vision mode)

## Mandatory Quality Gates

1. **Outcome Purity**:
   - **Pass**: Every strategic objective describes a measurable change in state, service quality, capability, or public outcome. Disguised projects are redirected to `developing-and-managing-strategy` (Ideas mode).
   - **Fail**: Objectives list deliverables, software tools, or tasks ("launch portal", "migrate database").
2. **Actionable Operating Principles**:
   - **Pass**: Each principle includes Statement, Rationale, and concrete Implications that can reject misaligned ideas.
   - **Fail**: Principles are vague slogans ("Excellence", "Collaboration") with no decision trade-off.
3. **Traceable Value Proposition**:
   - **Pass**: Benefits are mapped across public, service, operational, workforce, and risk dimensions without fabricating baseline numbers.
   - **Fail**: Benefits rely on generic claims ("will save 50% time") with no evidence or assumption tag.
