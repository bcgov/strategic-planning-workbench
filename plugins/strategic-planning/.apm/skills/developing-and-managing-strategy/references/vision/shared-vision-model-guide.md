# Shared Vision Model Guide

This guide describes how to coach and co-design a **Shared Vision Model Canvas** for public-sector initiatives, major transformation programs, and branch strategic plans in British Columbia.

---

## 1. Concept and Purpose

The Shared Vision Model is a single-page strategic communication canvas adapted from Geoffrey Moore's positioning framework for public-sector and justice-sector modernization initiatives. It connects target audiences, business needs, solution outputs, investment outcomes, strategic mandates, and AS-IS/TO-BE operating states into a single coherent narrative.

It is particularly valuable for:
- Executive business cases (such as the Court Administration Transformation Strategy / CATS);
- Multi-stakeholder transformation programs involving judicial, operational, and public participants;
- Communicating program scope clearly to oversight bodies, treasury boards, and frontline teams;
- Bridging high-level organizational mandates with concrete project outputs and measurable outcomes.

## 2. The Eight Strategic Dimensions

```text
┌────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│                          Shared Vision Canvas                          │
├──────────────────────────┬─────────────────────────────┬───────────────┤
│ For: [Target Audiences]  │ Who Need: [Specific Needs]  │ = Needs Scope │
├──────────────────────────┴─────────────────────────────┼───────────────┤
│ This: [Program / Modernization Vehicle]                │ = Solution    │
├────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┼───────────────┤
│ Through: [Project Outputs / Digital Applications]      │ = Outputs     │
├────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┼───────────────┤
│ Will Enable: [Investment Outcomes (Increases/Decreases)]│ = Outcomes   │
├────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┼───────────────┤
│ Which Support: [Sector Goals / Ministry Service Plan]  │ = Sector      │
├────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┼───────────────┤
│ In Alignment With: [Government Corporate Priorities]   │ = Corporate   │
├────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┼───────────────┤
│ Unlike: [Current State AS-IS Friction & Delays]        │ = Baseline    │
├────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┼───────────────┤
│ So As To Be: [Future State TO-BE Operating Model]      │ = Target      │
└────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┴───────────────┘
```

### Dimension 1: For (Target Groups)
- **Citizens / Public**: Direct service users, litigants, accused, victims, witnesses, self-represented parties, and general public.
- **Frontline Practitioners & Operational Managers**: Registry staff, court clerks, sheriff service, Crown counsel, private defence bar, law enforcement.
- **Judicial Officers & Governance Bodies**: Provincial Court Judges, Supreme Court Justices, Judicial Justices, Justices of the Peace, oversight committees.

### Dimension 2: Who Have These Needs
- Capture the distinct operational and service requirements of each group.
- Focus on practical frictions: timely resolution, reducing manual data re-entry, scheduling conflicts, lack of digital visibility, and paper handling.

### Dimension 3: This (Program Identity)
- The name and essence of the transformation vehicle (e.g., "The Court Administration Transformation Initiative").
- Frame it as an interconnected ecosystem of systems and processes, rather than a single isolated software install.

### Dimension 4: Through (Project Outputs)
- Controllable deliverables, applications, and procedural instruments.
- Examples: Court Clerk Desktop, Judicial Desktop, coordinated scheduling engines, digital exhibit repositories, modernized accounting tools.

### Dimension 5: Will Enable (Investment Outcomes)
- Partition clearly into:
  - **Measurable Increases**: Data flow velocity, real-time business intelligence, self-service adoption, user satisfaction.
  - **Measurable Decreases**: Court appearance volume, processing cycle time, manual rework, administrative overhead, case backlogs.

### Dimension 6: Which Support (Sector Outcomes) & In Alignment With (Corporate Outcomes)
- Upward line of sight to Ministry Service Plan goals and Provincial strategic priorities (e.g., improving access and outcomes to justice, reconciliation, regulatory compliance).

### Dimension 7: Unlike (Current State AS-IS)
- The burning platform: fragmented silos, information lost across institutional boundaries, paper dependence, duplicate entry, scheduling delays.

### Dimension 8: So As To Be (Target State TO-BE)
- The desired operational reality: integrated, transparent, timely, and citizen-centred justice delivery grounded in evidence-based management.

## 3. Interactive Coaching Protocol

When facilitating a Shared Vision session, ask questions in structured pairs:

1. **Audiences & Needs**:
   - *"Who are the main groups this transformation serves, and what is the single biggest friction each group experiences today?"*
2. **Current State & Target State**:
   - *"If we do nothing, what continues to break across handoffs? What will daily operations feel like when this is fully realized in 3–5 years?"*
3. **Outputs & Measurable Outcomes**:
   - *"What specific applications or services will be built, and what will measurably increase or decrease once people use them?"*
4. **Mandate Alignment**:
   - *"Which Ministry Service Plan objective and Provincial priority does this directly reinforce?"*

## 4. Relationship to Other Skills

- **`developing-and-managing-strategy` (Vision mode)**: Hosts the Shared Vision Model canvas and synthesized pitch.
- **`developing-and-managing-strategy` (Capabilities mode)**: Takes the outputs and maps the required organizational and technical capabilities (`CAP-X`) needed to sustain them.
- **`designing-logic-models`**: Decomposes the "Through (Outputs)" and "Will Enable (Outcomes)" into a full causal chain (Inputs ➔ Activities ➔ Outputs ➔ Immediate ➔ Intermediate ➔ Strategic Outcomes).
- **`developing-and-managing-strategy` (Work mode)**: Promotes the specific project outputs into committed work items (`WORK-X`) with assigned teams.
- **`rendering-strategic-visuals-and-decks`**: Visualizes the canvas into executive slide decks or B.C. Gov Design System HTML cards.
