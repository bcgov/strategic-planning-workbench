# Vision, principles, objectives and benefits guide

## 1. Interactive coaching flow for non-developers

1. **Target future state prompt**:
   - *"Imagine it is 3 years from now and your team's strategy has succeeded. In plain terms, what is materially different for (a) the people using your services, and (b) your own staff?"*
   - Offer 3 draft vision options (e.g., Option 1: Service- and access-focused, Option 2: Capability- and modernization-focused, Option 3: Balanced hybrid) so the user can react rather than stare at a blank page.
2. **Activity-to-outcome interactive reframing**:
   When a user says *"Our objective is to build an AI chatbot / new SharePoint intranet"*, respond with a supportive option menu:
   ```text
   Building a SharePoint intranet or AI tool is a great candidate initiative, which we can log in your Idea Inventory so it isn't lost.
   For your Strategic Objective, what outcome should that tool achieve?
   1. Enable registry staff and partners to find accurate procedural guidance at the point of need (Recommended)
   2. Reduce administrative preparation time while maintaining 100% policy and privacy compliance
   3. Both / let me describe the outcome in my own words
   ```
3. **Recap confirmation gate**:
   Always summarize the Vision Statement, 4–6 Operating Principles, and 3–6 Strategic Objectives (`OBJ-1`..`OBJ-N`) and ask for confirmation (`1. Save to strategy registers, 2. Adjust wording, 3. Generate Strategy on a Page preview`) before writing full documents.

## 2. Operating principles (TOGAF-light format)

Each principle requires three plain-language columns:
- **Principle statement**: Clear rule in sentence case (e.g., *Reuse existing branch capabilities before buying or building new tools*).
- **Rationale**: Why this matters to our mandate and stakeholders.
- **Concrete implication**: What we will start doing, stop doing, or decline when evaluating ideas.

## 3. Public-sector value dimensions

Map expected benefits across six public-sector value categories:
- **Public and democratic value**: Access to justice, procedural fairness, transparency, public trust, Indigenous reconciliation.
- **Service value**: Timeliness, plain-language clarity, accessibility, reduced citizen/litigant effort.
- **Operational value**: Consistency, reduced rework, faster cycle times.
- **Workforce value**: Reduced cognitive burden, clearer roles, sustainable workload.
- **Risk and compliance value**: Privacy (FOIPPA), security, statutory records integrity, reduced single-point-of-failure exposure.
- **Financial and stewardship value**: Shared capability reuse, cost avoidance, responsible public resource use.
