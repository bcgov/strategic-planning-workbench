# Multi-Tier Public-Sector Hierarchy Reference (BC Government / AG / CSB)

In the British Columbia public sector, a team's strategy nests inside four higher organizational tiers. Maintaining explicit visibility of each tier prevents local team plans from drifting out of alignment with branch or ministry direction.

## The Five Organizational Tiers

| Tier | Level | Example Entity (BC Justice Context) | Typical Strategic Artifacts | Key Questions for Our Team |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Tier 1** | **Province-Wide (Government of BC)** | Executive Council, Office of the Premier, OCIO / CITZ | Mandate Letters, BC Strategic Plan, BC Digital Plan, DRIPA Action Plan, Accessibility legislation | What government-wide commitments (reconciliation, digital accessibility, fiscal stewardship) constrain or guide us? |
| **Tier 2** | **Ministry Level** | **Ministry of Attorney General (AG)** | Ministry Service Plan, Justice & Public Safety Sector strategy, Access to Justice priorities | How does our work advance fair, timely access to justice while respecting judicial independence? |
| **Tier 3** | **Branch Level** | **Court Services Branch (CSB)** | CSB Strategic Plan, Court Administration & Registry Modernization, Digital Court Services, Sheriff Services | Which branch-wide strategic pillars and shared capabilities does our division/team contribute to? |
| **Tier 4** | **Division / Directorate Level** | CSB Division / Executive Leadership (e.g., Chris, Jessica) | Divisional strategic priorities, operational mandates, budget/capacity envelopes | What has divisional leadership explicitly confirmed vs. what are we interpreting? |
| **Tier 5** | **Team / Unit Level** | Our Team / Unit | Team Strategic Vision Brief, Strategy on a Page, Capability Map, Idea Inventory, Work Inventory, Measures | What are our specific outcome objectives, capabilities, ideas, and committed work items? |

## Line-of-Sight Traceability Chain

Every Team Strategic Objective (`OBJ-X`) should record its upward line of sight:
`Tier 5 Team Objective (OBJ-X)` $\rightarrow$ `Tier 4 Divisional Priority` $\rightarrow$ `Tier 3 CSB Strategic Pillar` $\rightarrow$ `Tier 2 AG Ministry Goal` $\rightarrow$ `Tier 1 BC Gov Mandate`.
