# Success measures guide

Owned by `developing-and-managing-strategy`. Writes only `## Success measures (MSR-)` in register `10`; the roadmap section belongs to `developing-and-managing-strategy`. Link each measure to an objective (`OBJ-X`) and, where relevant, to the work item (`WORK-X`) that should move it.

## Three-tier success measurement architecture

Borrowing from OKR best practices and public-sector performance measurement, every Strategic Objective requires three complementary measure types:

1. **Outcome Measures (Lagging)**: Did we achieve the intended change for service users, the public, or the branch? *(e.g., "Reduction in time required for registry staff to locate authoritative procedural guidance")*
2. **Leading Indicators (Early Signals)**: Are early behaviors and capability adoption moving in the right direction? *(e.g., "Percentage of high-volume procedures curated with verified owners and review dates")*
3. **Guardrail Measures (Quality & Safety Bounds)**: What must **not** degrade while we pursue speed or efficiency? *(e.g., "Zero privacy/FOIPPA breaches; 100% citation accuracy to authoritative policy; no increase in frontline rework")*

> **Evidence Rule**: Where a baseline number is unknown today, record `Baseline Needed (log in Evidence Gaps)` rather than inventing a placeholder percentage.
