# Acceptance criteria: `developing-and-managing-strategy` (Measures mode)

## Mandatory quality gates

1. **Triad measurement**
   - **Pass**: Each objective pairs an outcome measure and leading indicators with a guardrail measure, and marks missing baselines `Baseline needed`.
   - **Fail**: Invents baselines or targets, or omits quality, fairness or privacy guardrails.
2. **Section ownership respected**
   - **Pass**: Only the success measures section of register `10` is written.
   - **Fail**: The roadmap section is edited, or the section headings are changed.
