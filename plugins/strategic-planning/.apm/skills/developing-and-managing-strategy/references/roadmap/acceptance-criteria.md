# Acceptance criteria: `developing-and-managing-strategy` (Roadmap mode)

## Mandatory quality gates

1. **Commitment discipline**
   - **Pass**: Only `WORK-X` items with sponsor, owner and confirmed capacity appear as committed; `IDEA-X` items and approved-but-uncapacitated items appear only as decision gates, flagged `Capacity unconfirmed` where relevant.
   - **Fail**: An unapproved idea, or an item with no confirmed capacity, is shown as committed delivery.
2. **Dependencies and gates explicit**
   - **Pass**: Prerequisite capabilities, cross-ministry dependencies and assumptions to validate before each horizon change are shown.
   - **Fail**: Initiatives are listed in parallel with no sequencing or capacity awareness.
3. **Section ownership respected**
   - **Pass**: Only the roadmap and decision gates section of register `10` is written; measures are left to Measures mode.
   - **Fail**: The success measures section is edited, or the section headings are changed.
