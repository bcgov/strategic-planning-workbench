# Acceptance criteria: `developing-and-managing-strategy` (Capabilities mode)

## Mandatory Quality Gates

1. **Solution-Agnostic Naming**:
   - **Pass**: Capabilities are expressed as stable organizational abilities (what the branch does), free of vendor names, software products, or temporary project titles.
   - **Fail**: Capabilities list tools or projects ("Build PowerBI Dashboard", "Deploy Azure OpenAI").
2. **Five-Pillar Gap Analysis**:
   - **Pass**: Every capability gap assesses People/Skills, Process, Information/Data, Technology, and Policy/Governance.
   - **Fail**: Capability gaps only describe software or infrastructure purchases.
3. **Strategic Traceability**:
   - **Pass**: Every capability links to one or more Strategic Objectives (`OBJ-X`) and specifies a disposition (`Strengthen`, `Create`, `Share/Reuse`, or `Partner`).
   - **Fail**: Capabilities float without connection to strategic objectives.
