# Acceptance criteria: `developing-and-managing-strategy`

Each mode enforces its own detailed gate under `references/<mode>/acceptance-criteria.md`. Across all modes:

1. **Workspace & bookkeeping**: Target `strategy/` folder resolved before creating files; only owned register sections edited; IDs preserved; `changelog.md` and `00-planning-session-status.md` (`Open follow-ups`) updated.
2. **Three-inventory separation**: Strategic vision, `IDEA-X` candidates, and `WORK-X` commitments remain strictly separate; work without a named sponsor, approval date, owner, and confirmed capacity stays a decision gate (`Capacity unconfirmed`).
3. **Evidence & style**: `Confirmed` vs. `Interpreted` tags preserved; no fabricated baselines; B.C. Government sentence case, plain language, and zero emoji.
