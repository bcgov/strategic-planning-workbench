# Acceptance criteria: `developing-and-managing-strategy` (Work mode)

## Mandatory Quality Gates

1. **No Unapproved Ideas in Work Inventory**:
   - **Pass**: Every item has verified commitment (Sponsor, Owner, Status) or is explicitly flagged as `Shadow Operation / Unverified Commitment` requiring governance review. Unapproved proposals are routed to Ideas mode.
   - **Fail**: Brainstormed ideas or wishlist items are added as active work items.
2. **Complete Strategic Traceability Fields**:
   - **Pass**: Each work item records `Strategic Objective Supported`, `Capability Developed`, `Expected Outcome`, `Success Measures`, and `Related Idea ID` (or `Unaligned / Orphaned` when missing).
   - **Fail**: Work items are listed only as task names without strategic linkage or ownership.
3. **Operational Load Visibility**:
   - **Pass**: Distinguishes bounded initiatives and time-boxed experiments from ongoing operational responsibilities.
   - **Fail**: Treats permanent operational duties or open-ended prototypes as one-off projects.
