## 3. Evaluation Criteria & Weighting Configuration

### Value / Return on Investment (Weight: [50%])
- **Drivers ([25%])**: Urgency driven by legislation, mandate, or critical operational risk.
- **Strategic Alignment ([25%])**: Alignment with ministry service plan and provincial priorities.
- **Outcomes & Benefits ([25%])**: Direct citizen benefits, operational efficiencies, and cost savings.
- **Sector Benefits ([25%])**: Cross-agency reusability and common platforms.

### Ability to Deliver (Weight: [50%])
- **Organizational Readiness ([20%])**: Staff bandwidth, change capacity, and skills.
- **Extent of Research ([20%])**: Depth of discovery, service design, and evidence.
- **Investment Cost ([20%])**: Affordability, capital certainty, and funding availability.
- **Executive Support ([20%])**: Senior leadership sponsorship and governance backing.
- **Ease of Implementation ([20%])**: Architectural simplicity and low delivery friction.
