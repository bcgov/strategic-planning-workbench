# Acceptance Criteria: `sequencing-roadmaps-and-measures`

## Mandatory Quality Gates

1. **Strict Commitment Discipline on Roadmaps**:
   - **Pass**: Only verified `WORK-X` items are shown as committed roadmap initiatives; unapproved `IDEA-X` items appear solely as explicit `Decision / Validation Gates`.
   - **Fail**: Mixes unapproved brainstormed ideas into the roadmap as committed delivery items.
2. **Dependencies & Decision Gates Explicit**:
   - **Pass**: Shows prerequisite capabilities, cross-ministry dependencies, and assumptions that must be validated before transitioning between horizons.
   - **Fail**: Lists initiatives in parallel without sequencing dependencies or capacity awareness.
3. **Triad Measurement (Outcome + Leading + Guardrail)**:
   - **Pass**: Each objective pairs Outcome and Leading indicators with a Guardrail measure, and marks missing baselines honestly as `Baseline Needed`.
   - **Fail**: Invents fake baseline/target percentages or omits quality/fairness/privacy guardrails.
