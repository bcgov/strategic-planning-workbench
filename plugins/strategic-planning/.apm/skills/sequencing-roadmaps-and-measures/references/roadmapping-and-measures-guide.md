# Strategic Roadmapping & Success Measures Guide

## 1. Horizon Sequencing (`Now / Next / Later` & `Three Horizons`)

Strategic roadmaps communicate **sequencing, prerequisites, and decision gates**—not false-precision dates for work 18 months away.

| Horizon | Focus | What Belongs Here |
|---|---|---|
| **Horizon 1 — Now (Current Window)** | Foundational capabilities, active committed initiatives (`WORK-X`), and urgent evidence-gap closures. | Committed work with confirmed owners, sponsors, and active capacity. |
| **Horizon 2 — Next (Near-Term)** | Capability building and validated candidate transitions unlocked by Horizon 1 foundations. | Sequenced initiatives plus explicit **Decision Gates** for `candidate` ideas (`IDEA-X`). |
| **Horizon 3 — Later (Long-Term)** | Strategic transformation and multi-partner capabilities dependent on policy, funding, or platform maturity. | High-level capability horizons and directional options—never detailed task lists. |

## 2. Prioritizing Initiatives After Strategy Is Established

Once Strategic Objectives (`OBJ-X`) and Capabilities (`CAP-X`) are locked, prioritize initiatives using:
- **Dependency Order**: Foundational capabilities (e.g., authoritative knowledge taxonomy, governance guardrails) must precede downstream automation or AI consumption.
- **Public-Sector Value vs. Effort/Risk**: Weigh public/service value and risk reduction against organizational capacity and policy complexity.

## 3. Three-Tier Success Measurement Architecture

Borrowing from OKR best practices and public-sector performance measurement, every Strategic Objective requires three complementary measure types:

1. **Outcome Measures (Lagging)**: Did we achieve the intended change for service users, the public, or the branch? *(e.g., "Reduction in time required for registry staff to locate authoritative procedural guidance")*
2. **Leading Indicators (Early Signals)**: Are early behaviors and capability adoption moving in the right direction? *(e.g., "Percentage of high-volume procedures curated with verified owners and review dates")*
3. **Guardrail Measures (Quality & Safety Bounds)**: What must **not** degrade while we pursue speed or efficiency? *(e.g., "Zero privacy/FOIPPA breaches; 100% citation accuracy to authoritative policy; no increase in frontline rework")*

> **Evidence Rule**: Where a baseline number is unknown today, record `Baseline Needed (log in Evidence Gaps)` rather than inventing a placeholder percentage.
