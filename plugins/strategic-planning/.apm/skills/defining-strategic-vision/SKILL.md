# Defining strategic vision

Guides teams through an interactive conversation to establish a shared strategic vision, future-state operating principles, outcome-oriented strategic objectives, and expected public/business value.

## Contents

- [Constraints](#constraints)
- [Quick start](#quick-start)
- [Workflow](#workflow)

- [References](#references)

## Constraints

1. **Interactive coaching**: Ask 1–2 plain-language questions at a time. When a user proposes an activity or software tool as an objective, gently coach them to uncover the underlying outcome using a numbered choice menu.
2. **Outcomes over activities**: Reframe disguised projects or technology solutions into measurable service or operational outcomes, and offer to log the specific solution idea in `managing-idea-inventory`.
3. **B.C. Government Design System style**: Use sentence case for all headings and labels, calm plain language, zero emoji, and zero exclamation marks.
4. **Recap confirmation gate**: Present a concise recap of the vision, principles, and objectives before generating or saving full briefs.

## Quick start

1. Read [vision-and-objectives-guide.md](references/vision-and-objectives-guide.md) for interactive coaching prompts and the activity-to-outcome test.
2. Walk the user through target future state, operating principles, outcome objectives, and expected value with a recap confirmation gate.
3. Populate [strategy-on-a-page.md](assets/templates/strategy-on-a-page.md), [strategic-vision-brief.md](assets/templates/strategic-vision-brief.md), and [benefits-dependency-map.md](assets/templates/benefits-dependency-map.md).

## Workflow

1. **Interactive Future-State Discovery**: Ask what will look and feel materially different in 3–5 years for service users, frontline staff, and leadership/partners. Offer 2–3 draft vision statements for the user to choose or blend.
2. **Co-Design Operating Principles**: Propose 4–6 candidate operating principles (each with Statement, Rationale, and Trade-Off Implication) and ask the user which ones fit their branch reality.
3. **Coach Strategic Objectives (Outcome Test)**: Draft 3–6 outcome objectives (`OBJ-X`). If any input is a disguised activity, explain why and offer outcome reframings while routing the activity to `managing-idea-inventory`.
4. **Map Public & Operational Value**: Connect each objective to public, service, operational, workforce, risk, and stewardship benefits in [benefits-dependency-map.md](assets/templates/benefits-dependency-map.md).
5. **Recap Gate & Hand Off**: Confirm the [strategy-on-a-page.md](assets/templates/strategy-on-a-page.md) summary and offer next steps (`planning-business-capabilities` or `rendering-strategic-visuals-and-decks`).

## Verification

Verify outputs against [acceptance-criteria.md](references/acceptance-criteria.md):
- Disguised activities are interactively reframed into outcomes with user confirmation.
- Headings and tables follow B.C. Government sentence case with zero emoji.
