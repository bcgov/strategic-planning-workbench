# Court Administration Transformation Initiative (CATS) Shared Vision Model

> **Status**: Approved Reference Business Case Example  
> **Owning Organization**: Ministry of Justice / Court Services Branch (CSB)  
> **Strategic Context**: B.C. Justice Reform White Paper (October 2012) & Court Administration Transformation Strategy (CATS)  
> **Format**: B.C. Government Public Sector Shared Vision Model Canvas

---

## 1. Executive Shared Vision Canvas

| Strategic Dimension | Audience & Solution Scope | Alignment Focus & Operational Impact |
|---|---|---|
| **For (Target Groups)** | **The 4.3 Million Citizens of British Columbia**:<br/>• Direct justice clients including complainants, victims, witnesses, litigants, legal counsel, accused persons, prisoners, offenders, parolees, and broader justice stakeholders.<br/><br/>**Frontline Public-Safety & Justice Service Providers and Managers**:<br/>• Law enforcement agencies<br/>• Crown counsel<br/>• Defence bar<br/>• Civil litigators<br/>• Court administration staff<br/><br/>**The Judiciary**:<br/>• Provincial Court Judges<br/>• Supreme Court Justices<br/>• Appeal Court Justices<br/>• Other Judicial Officers (e.g., Judicial Justices, Court Services Justices of the Peace) | Multi-audience scope covering citizens seeking justice, operational practitioners conducting justice business, and judicial decision-makers adjudicating matters. |
| **Who Have These Needs** | **Citizen Needs**:<br/>• Access to justice in a way that is timely and engenders confidence in the justice system through the provision of effective, fair, and efficient justice services.<br/><br/>**Frontline Practitioner & Manager Needs**:<br/>• Enabling systems that take advantage of modern technologies in order to:<br/>  - Effect enhanced business processes;<br/>  - Acquire necessary information and documents without delay;<br/>  - Maximize the effective use of available court resources;<br/>  - Reduce process error; and<br/>  - Make better-informed decisions that lead to better justice.<br/><br/>**Judicial Needs**:<br/>• A more effective way to:<br/>  - Manage and schedule judicial resources more efficiently and to greatest effect;<br/>  - Access documents, administrative information, and legal references to make more timely and better-informed decisions; and<br/>  - Monitor and manage system performance through operational business intelligence and reporting tools. | Identifies specific pain points and functional requirements across the three primary stakeholder tiers of the justice sector. |
| **This (Program Identity)** | **Court Administration Transformation Initiative (CATS)**:<br/>The development and implementation of a series of applications that leverage previous and current investments to create an ecosystem of interconnected business systems and processes across court operations. | High-level transformation vehicle establishing an integrated digital justice ecosystem. |
| **Through (Project Outputs)** | **Interconnected Application Components**:<br/>1. **Court Clerk Desktop (CCD)**: Integrates civil and criminal case tracking systems with the electronic court record.<br/>2. **Provincial Court Scheduling Project (PCSP)**: Supports the new integrated scheduling model.<br/>3. **Judicial Desktop**: Enables the judiciary to work electronically to access court files, rotas, schedules, exhibits, and bench references.<br/>4. **Court Services Scheduling**: Application supporting scheduling of Court Services Branch in-court resources based on availability.<br/>5. **Crown Scheduling**: Application supporting Crown file ownership by identifying Crown availability during scheduling.<br/>6. **External Counsel & Self-Represented Scheduling**: External-facing web application enabling private counsel and self-represented litigants to perform remote scheduling.<br/>7. **In-Court Exhibit Management & Evidence Presentation**: Application for consistent and efficient management of physical and electronic exhibits and evidence.<br/>8. **Courts Fees and Fines Accounting System**: Modernized replacement of legacy financial applications integrated across the justice suite.<br/>9. **Superior Courts Management Information System (SC-MIS)**: Reporting and analytics platform supporting strategic planning, business intelligence, and performance measurement. | Controllable project deliverables that together create the digital court administration infrastructure. |
| **Will Enable (Investment Outcomes)** | **Measurable Increases in**:<br/>• Seamless transfer of electronic information and data between justice participants, managers, and partners.<br/>• Capability to monitor, measure, and refine system performance using operational business intelligence.<br/>• Provision of synchronized, real-time information to all justice administrators and participants to support faster, higher-quality decisions.<br/>• System transparency through public and operational performance reporting to uncover further efficiencies.<br/>• Connected business process and document flows across independent justice institutions.<br/>• New digital service channels for citizens expanding timely access to justice.<br/>• Fine and fee recoveries through integrated accounting and payment channels.<br/><br/>**Measurable Decreases in**:<br/>• The number of court appearances, supporting process transformations that enable fewer, more meaningful court appearances. | Investment results, operational improvements, and public value generated by using the application suite. |
| **Which Support (Strategic Sector Outcomes)** | **Ministry of Justice Service Plan**:<br/>• Balanced, accessible, and efficient justice processes and services across British Columbia. | Direct upward contribution to ministry-level operational and service goals. |
| **In Alignment With (Corporate Outcomes)** | **Government Strategic Priorities**:<br/>• Improving access and outcomes to justice for all British Columbians. | Alignment with provincial cabinet priorities and cross-government direction. |
| **Unlike (Current State AS-IS)** | **Disparate and Compartmentalized Justice Architecture**:<br/>A fragmented operating environment where efficiencies gained in one business area are lost when information transfers to another part of the justice system, causing systemic delays, handoff errors, and inefficient use of scarce justice resources. | Baseline friction and operational failure modes driving the transformation case. |
| **So As To Be (Target State TO-BE)** | **A Modern, Balanced, and Citizen-Centred Justice System**:<br/>Full support for government's commitment to a transparent, timely, and balanced justice system as articulated in the October 2012 White Paper on Justice Reform, realizing:<br/>• Clear performance focus and empirical accountability;<br/>• Efficient case management practices;<br/>• Integrated inter-agency approaches;<br/>• Evidence-based programs grounded in what works; and<br/>• Accessible, citizen-focused justice services. | The target operational end-state where cross-institutional value is realized. |

## 2. Synthesized Narrative Vision Pitch

> **For** the 4.3 million citizens of British Columbia, frontline justice practitioners, and the Judiciary,  
> **Who need** timely, accessible, and trusted justice supported by modern electronic workflows, transparent scheduling, and reliable real-time case information,  
> **The Court Administration Transformation Initiative (CATS)**  
> **Is a** multi-year modernization program establishing an ecosystem of interconnected business systems and collaborative processes  
> **That through** Court Clerk Desktop, Judicial Desktop, coordinated scheduling applications, digital exhibit management, and modern financial and BI analytics tools,  
> **Will enable** seamless data exchange, unified scheduling, real-time performance intelligence, and fewer, more meaningful court appearances,  
> **Which supports** the Ministry of Justice commitment to balanced, accessible, and efficient justice services in alignment with government priorities for improving access and outcomes to justice.  
> **Unlike** today's disparate, compartmentalized systems where efficiencies in one area are lost across organizational handoffs,  
> **Our future state will** deliver the transparent, timely, integrated, and citizen-centred justice system envisioned in the B.C. Justice Reform White Paper.
