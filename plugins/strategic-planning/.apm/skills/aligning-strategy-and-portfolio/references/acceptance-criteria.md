# Acceptance Criteria: `aligning-strategy-and-portfolio`

## Mandatory Quality Gates

1. **Complete Chain Traceability**:
   - **Pass**: Maps `Leadership Direction` $\rightarrow$ `Team Vision` $\rightarrow$ `Objectives` $\rightarrow$ `Capabilities` $\rightarrow$ `Ideas` $\rightarrow$ `Committed Work` $\rightarrow$ `Measures`, explicitly highlighting every broken link.
   - **Fail**: Forces fake alignment links or hides orphaned projects and unsupported objectives.
2. **All 8 Diagnostic Conditions Audited**:
   - **Pass**: Explicitly reports findings across all 8 alignment conditions (orphaned work, unsupported objectives, unaddressed capabilities, stalled ideas, duplicate ideas, duplicate work, shadow ops, and unapproved ideas treated as work).
   - **Fail**: Performs only a superficial keyword match without portfolio hygiene diagnostics.
3. **Comprehensive Risk Coverage**:
   - **Pass**: Evaluates strategic, policy/legislative, privacy/security, workforce, and dependency risks with concrete mitigations and owners.
   - **Fail**: Lists only generic technical schedule risks.
