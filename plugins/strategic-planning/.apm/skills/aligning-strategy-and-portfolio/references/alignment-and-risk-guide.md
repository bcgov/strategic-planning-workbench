# Strategy Alignment & Portfolio Risk Guide

## 1. The 8 Alignment Diagnostic Conditions

When auditing traceability across the Strategic Vision, Capability Map, Idea Inventory, and Work Inventory, systematically test for these eight conditions:

| # | Diagnostic Condition | What It Means | Recommended Action |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Orphaned Active Work** | Work underway (`WORK-X`) with no clear link to any Strategic Objective (`OBJ-X`) or Capability (`CAP-X`). | Justify as mandatory operational baseline, realign to an objective, or wind down/transfer. |
| 2 | **Unsupported Strategic Objective** | A Strategic Objective (`OBJ-X`) with zero supporting active work or validated candidate ideas. | Prioritize targeted ideation/discovery or revise the objective's horizon. |
| 3 | **Unaddressed Capability Gap** | A critical capability (`CAP-X`) required by the vision that is absent from both the Idea and Work Inventories. | Flag as a critical portfolio blind spot; commission discovery. |
| 4 | **Unevaluated High-Potential Ideas** | Ideas aligned to high-priority capability gaps that have stalled in `raw` or `explored` status. | Schedule assumption-testing and validation. |
| 5 | **Duplicate / Competing Ideas** | Multiple ideas (`IDEA-X`, `IDEA-Y`) addressing the same opportunity or capability gap independently. | Combine into a single stronger candidate or compare head-to-head. |
| 6 | **Duplicate Active Work** | Separate sub-teams or branches building overlapping solutions (`WORK-X`, `WORK-Y`) for the same capability. | Consolidate into a shared capability or clarify scope boundaries. |
| 7 | **Quietly Operationalized Experiments** | Time-boxed pilots or proofs of concept that have drifted into daily operational reliance without support governance. | Trigger formal promotion gate review or decommission plan. |
| 8 | **Ideas Treated as Commitments** | Unapproved ideas appearing in stakeholder updates or roadmaps as if they were funded, committed projects. | Move back to Idea Inventory and correct stakeholder messaging. |

## 2. Public-Sector Strategic Risk Taxonomy

Evaluate portfolio and strategic risks across seven lenses:
1. **Strategic & Mandate Risk**: Drift from ministerial/executive direction or conflicting stakeholder expectations.
2. **Policy, Legislative & Procedural Fairness Risk**: Actions that conflict with statutes, court rules, or administrative fairness.
3. **Privacy, Security & Records Risk**: Unauthorized data exposure, FOIPPA non-compliance, or unapproved AI data handling.
4. **Judicial & Operational Independence Risk**: Blurring executive administration with judicial decision-making.
5. **Workforce & Change Readiness Risk**: Change fatigue, key-person dependency, or lack of operational support capacity.
6. **Cross-Ministry & Partner Dependency Risk**: Reliance on external ministries, central IT, or justice partners without agreed service levels.
7. **Vendor & Platform Lock-In Risk**: Over-dependence on proprietary tooling or unapproved shadow IT.
