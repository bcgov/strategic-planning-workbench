# Managing idea inventory

Guides users interactively to maintain the **Idea inventory** (*"What might we do?"*) separately from the Strategic vision and the committed Work inventory, moving ideas through a 7-stage funnel (`Capture` $\rightarrow$ `Clarify` $\rightarrow$ `Align` $\rightarrow$ `Validate` $\rightarrow$ `Compare` $\rightarrow$ `Decide` $\rightarrow$ `Commit`).

## Constraints

1. **Interactive funnel coaching**: Present a numbered menu so the user can choose whether to quickly capture raw ideas, brainstorm/stress-test an idea's assumptions, or evaluate validated candidates for promotion.
2. **Ideas are NOT committed work**: Never represent an idea, suggestion, or unapproved experiment as committed work. Keep it in the Idea inventory until sponsorship, ownership, and capacity are confirmed.
3. **Allow incomplete early ideas**: Do not force premature scoring on `raw` or `explored` ideas when evidence does not yet exist.
4. **B.C. Government Design System style**: Use sentence case for headings and labels, plain language, zero emoji, and a recap confirmation gate before saving.

## Quick start

1. Read [idea-funnel-and-evaluation-guide.md](references/idea-funnel-and-evaluation-guide.md) and present the **4-option Idea Mode Menu** (1. Quick-capture raw ideas, 2. Clarify & align an idea, 3. Stress-test assumptions, 4. Compare candidates for promotion).
2. Capture and refine ideas in [idea-inventory.md](assets/templates/idea-inventory.md) and [idea-record.md](assets/templates/idea-record.md).
3. Evaluate `validated` / `candidate` ideas using [idea-evaluation.md](assets/templates/idea-evaluation.md).

## Workflow

1. **Interactive Capture (`raw`)**: Ask for the idea title, source, and problem/opportunity in plain language—allowing incomplete fields without judgment.
2. **Clarify & Align (`explored`)**: Ask 1–2 questions to separate the underlying problem from the proposed solution and link to `OBJ-X` and `CAP-X`.
3. **Stress-Test Assumptions (`validated`)**: Walk the user through the Riskiest Assumption Test (policy, privacy, judicial/operational fit, capacity) and suggest a small validation step.
4. **Compare & Decide (`candidate` / `deferred` / `rejected`)**: Present a comparison table using [idea-evaluation.md](assets/templates/idea-evaluation.md) (or route to `prioritizing-and-scoring-investments` for weighted 4-quadrant scoring) and ask the user to choose a disposition (`Promote`, `Explore`, `Defer`, `Combine`, `Reject`).
5. **Recap & Promotion Gate**: Confirm the recap before updating `strategy/07-idea-inventory.md`; if promoted with confirmed sponsor/owner, chain to `managing-work-inventory`.
