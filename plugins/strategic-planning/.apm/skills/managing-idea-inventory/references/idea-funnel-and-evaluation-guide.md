# Idea Funnel, Brainstorming & Evaluation Guide

## 1. The 7-Stage Idea Promotion Funnel

| Stage | Maturity State | Core Question | Allowed Actions |
|---|---|---|---|
| **1. Capture** | `raw` | *What is the idea and who raised it?* | Log title, description, source, and initial problem/opportunity without premature scoring. |
| **2. Clarify** | `explored` | *What problem does this solve and for whom?* | Separate the underlying problem from the proposed solution; identify affected stakeholders. |
| **3. Align** | `explored` | *Which strategic objective and capability does it support?* | Map to `OBJ-X` and `CAP-X`. Flag orphan ideas that do not support the strategic vision. |
| **4. Validate** | `validated` | *What assumptions must be true for this to work?* | Run assumption testing; check legislative, privacy, security, and workforce constraints. |
| **5. Compare** | `candidate` | *How does this compare to alternative ideas and active work?* | Apply multi-criteria evaluation (Strategic Fit, Public/Service Value, Feasibility, Risk) only after validation. |
| **6. Decide** | `candidate` / `deferred` / `rejected` | *Should we promote, explore further, defer, combine, or reject?* | Record explicit decision rationale and date. |
| **7. Commit** | `promoted` | *Who sponsors and owns the committed work?* | Hand off to `managing-work-inventory` with `Related Idea ID` traceability. |

## 2. Strategic Brainstorming & Assumption-Testing Modes

Adapted from upstream strategic thinking patterns:
- **Problem vs. Solution Separation**: When someone proposes *"Let's build an AI tool for X"*, ask *"What friction or failure mode are staff or service users experiencing today, and what are 3 non-AI and AI ways to address that capability gap?"*
- **Riskiest Assumption Test (RAT)**: Identify the single assumption that, if false, invalidates the entire idea (e.g., statutory authority, data availability, judicial acceptance, or frontline adoption capacity) and design the cheapest way to test it before writing a business case.
